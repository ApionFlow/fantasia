Privacy

This is an English translation of the German privacy policy. If the two versions differ, the German version applies.

Controller and contact

Fantasia is provided by Michael Mühlberger. For questions about privacy, you can reach me at support@apionflow.com. Fantasia is a private dream journal without a user account. There is no advertising and no tracking. Journal entries are never transferred to the developer automatically.

What data Fantasia processes and why

The app processes the dream texts you type or dictate, titles, date and time, optional voice memos and images, marked terms, feelings, tags, notes and saved links, as well as your app settings. This data is used to save, display, edit, find and review your own entries. Term recognition, pattern overviews, dictation cleanup and moon phase calculation happen locally on your device. The developer does not receive the content of your journal automatically.

Storage and iCloud

Your entries are stored on your device. A dream you are capturing and have not saved yet is kept as a draft on this device only, so it is not lost if iOS closes the app. The draft is not synced and is deleted when you save or discard it. If iCloud is turned on for Fantasia, your journal data, including images and saved voice memos, is synced through Apple CloudKit in the private database of your iCloud account. The developer cannot see this private database. You can turn off iCloud for Fantasia in the system settings. The use of iCloud is also subject to Apple’s privacy policy.

Microphone and speech recognition

Before you dictate, Fantasia asks for the required system permissions. Recognition happens on the device where possible. With the older speech recognition method, Apple may process audio if on-device recognition is not available. Optionally, Fantasia keeps the original recording as a voice memo in your journal. Voice recordings are never sent to OpenAI. You can withdraw microphone and speech recognition permissions in the system settings.

Optional image generation: recipient and data

When you set up and start image generation, Fantasia communicates directly with the AI service OpenAI at api.openai.com over an encrypted HTTPS connection. Under OpenAI’s terms, the contracting party for API customers in the European Economic Area and Switzerland is OpenAI Ireland Ltd.; elsewhere, another OpenAI entity may be responsible.

If you write an image idea, only that idea is sent as personal text, limited to 2,500 characters. Without an image idea, the dream text is sent instead, limited to 6,000 characters. If you turn on “Match the style of the image shown”, the image currently shown for this dream is also sent as a style reference. Fantasia adds general design instructions and technical image parameters. Other journal entries and voice memos are never included.

The API key you entered is sent to OpenAI for authentication and links the request to your OpenAI account. The service also receives technically necessary connection data, such as your IP address. The key is stored locally in the device keychain and is not synced through iCloud. Using the API may cause charges in your OpenAI account.

Your consent to image generation

Before Fantasia sends anything to OpenAI for the first time, the app explains what will be sent — the image idea or, without one, the dream text, and with “Match the style of the image shown” turned on also the image shown — and previews the content of the first request. Only when you tap “Agree and create image” do you allow the transfer. Tapping “Cancel” or closing the sheet sends nothing. Your consent is then stored on this device and applies to further images; directly below the generate button, the app states each time what will be sent. Your consent is not synced through iCloud. You can withdraw it at any time under Settings → OpenAI image generation; Fantasia will then ask again before the next transfer. If what is sent, or to whom, ever changes, Fantasia also asks again. Saving an API key is not consent to transfer your content. Your journal remains fully usable without consent.

You can refuse future transfers at any time and remove the saved API key under Settings → OpenAI image generation. A request that has already been sent cannot be recalled.

Processing and protection at OpenAI

OpenAI processes the transferred content to create the image and to prevent abuse. According to OpenAI’s documentation, API content is not used for model training by default unless the API account holder opts in to data sharing. Safety logs may generally retain content for up to 30 days; legal and safety-related exceptions are possible. Fantasia does not opt in to data sharing for training and cannot guarantee immediate deletion at OpenAI.

To protect transferred personal data, OpenAI’s API terms and the data processing addendum they contain provide in particular for purpose limitation, confidentiality, technical and organisational security measures, and comparable obligations for subprocessors. These obligations protect further processing by the recipient. They do not mean that external processing is the same as purely local storage. Processing may also take place outside Switzerland and the EEA; the addendum describes the safeguards provided for such transfers. The terms and settings that apply to your API account are decisive.

Photos, links and support

Fantasia only asks for permission to add to Photos when you explicitly save an image there. You add your own images through the system photo picker: Fantasia only receives the photos you select and can neither see nor search the rest of your library. When added, images are resized and saved again without metadata — in particular without location and capture time. Like all images, they are stored on your device and in your private iCloud, and are only sent to OpenAI if you explicitly use one as a style reference. When you open a saved link or a privacy link, the website operator receives the usual connection data. If you contact support by email, your sender address, your message and any attachments you choose to send are processed to handle your request. Please only send the information your request needs.

Retention, deletion and your rights

Journal entries are kept until you delete them. You can remove individual dreams, voice memos and images in Fantasia. You also manage iCloud data and device backups in Apple’s system settings; images you exported to Photos are deleted there separately. If needed, remove your API key in Fantasia, because deleting the app does not reliably remove all keychain data.

Deleting an entry in Fantasia does not retroactively delete content already sent to OpenAI. For access or deletion requests about your OpenAI account, use OpenAI’s privacy contact. For data you sent to Fantasia support, contact support@apionflow.com. Support correspondence is only kept as long as needed to handle your request or to meet legal obligations. Depending on the applicable law, you have in particular the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, objection and to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.
