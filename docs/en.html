Fantasia

Capture & connect your dreams

Tell your dream before it fades. Fantasia captures it and, over time, shows you what keeps coming back in your dreams. No account, no ads.

Get it on the App Store Help & contact

Capture

Tap the microphone and tell it, or write it down. Keep the voice memo if you like.

Rediscover

Mark people, places and symbols. Fantasia recognizes what keeps returning in your own dreams.

Stay private

Your dreams live on your iPhone and, if you want, in your private iCloud.
